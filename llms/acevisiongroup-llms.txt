## What this company is

- Founded 2006 by AnnMarie Hipsley, DPT, PhD (Founder and CEO), a vision scientist and
  the inventor of the VisioLite ophthalmic laser system.
- Headquarters: One Boston Place, 201 Washington Street, Suite 2600, Boston, MA 02108.
- Product: the VisioLite Er:YAG Laser System (Alpha 2.0 configuration), a non-contact
  laser delivery architecture operating at 2940 nm.
- Procedure: Laser Scleral Microporation (LSM) — partial-thickness micropores in five
  scleral zones intended to reduce scleral cross-linking and restore the eye's dynamic
  range of focus.
- Regulatory status: the VisioLite Er:YAG Laser System is under development and has NOT
